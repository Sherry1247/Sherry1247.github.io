ASME Undergraduate Engineering Student Competition: Winner

ASME · Faculty advisor Saurabh K. Gupta

Won the competition with a residual-based framework that diagnoses sensor faults, estimates severity, and corrects the damaged signal.

Virtual SensingFault DiagnosisXGBoostSignal Correction

Presented the Virtual Sensor-Guided Noise Diagnosis and Self-Correction Framework for Sensor Health Monitoring, built on heavy-duty diesel engine data.

Highlights

  • 10-class XGBoost fault classifier: 98.74% accuracy and 98.56% macro F1.
  • Severity estimation and signal correction cut error by over 90% in representative bias and drift cases.

Conference

Central States Section of the Combustion Institute: Undergraduate Research Competition

Central States Section, The Combustion Institute

Presented a machine-learning virtual sensor for combustion monitoring, with Prof. Saurabh K. Gupta as faculty advisor.

Virtual SensingCombustion MonitoringANNPyTorch

Talk: Machine Learning–Based Virtual Sensor for Automotive Engine Combustion Monitoring. A single multi-target ANN estimates intake air mass flow, NOx emissions, and start of combustion from six ECU signals.

Highlights

  • Predictive R² of 0.9945 (intake air mass flow), 0.9891 (NOx), and 0.9841 (start of combustion).
  • Data from a 12 L six-cylinder heavy-duty diesel engine across 217 steady-state operating points.
